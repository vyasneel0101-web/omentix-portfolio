## What I want from you

Design the **visual direction** for this site as screens I can look at and compare. Priority order:

1. **Hero section** — the most important screen. Currently the weakest.
2. **Work / case-study section** — how the projects are presented.
3. **Contact / footer section**.
4. Optional: nav, and the loading screen.

Give me **2–3 genuinely different directions** for the hero, not three variations of one idea. I'd rather reject something bold than accept something safe.

## Hard constraints (please respect these)

- **Single scrolling page.** Not a multi-page site.
- There is a **scroll-scrubbed video centerpiece** between the hero and the work section. As the user scrolls, a hand-drawn ink illustration animates: a root system grows downward, becomes a tree, then resolves into the Omentix node logo. Text "beats" fade in over it at set points. **You don't need to build this** — just design *around* it, and design the text beats that sit on top of it (see below). Assume it's a full-viewport pinned section.
- **Real content only** — the copy and projects below are real. Don't invent fake testimonials, fake client logos, fake metrics, or fake team members.
- The site's artwork is **hand-drawn ink line-work on cream paper, with purple ink splatter and halftone dot textures**. Whatever you design should feel like it belongs next to that — organic and printed, not glassy or neon.

## Voice

Direct, specific, a little dry. Confident without hype. No "elevate your business", no "leverage", no "cutting-edge", no emoji. Concrete claims about real work.

---

## What "good" means here

Please avoid the usual AI-design tells: purple-blue gradients on text, centered-everything layouts, identical rounded cards in a 3-grid, generic icon sets, drop shadows doing the work of hierarchy.

I'd rather see: asymmetric editorial layout, real scale contrast, intentional whitespace, visible grid structure, type doing the heavy lifting, and one or two details that look like a person made a decision.
